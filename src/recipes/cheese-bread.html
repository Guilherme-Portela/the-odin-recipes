Cheese Bread


Back to home

Description

A traditional Brazilian snack made with tapioca flour and Parmesan cheese. These gluten-free cheese bread rolls are crispy on the outside and soft and cheesy on the inside — perfect for breakfast or an afternoon snack.

Info

  • Total Time: 40 min
  • Servings: 6
  • Yield: 6 rolls

Ingredients

  • ½ cup olive oil or butter
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 1/3 cup milk or soy milk
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups tapioca flour
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • 2/3 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 beaten eggs

Recipe

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Mix the olive oil, water, milk, and salt in a large saucepan and place over high heat.
  2. Bring to a boil and immediately remove from the heat.
  3. Stir in the tapioca flour and garlic until smooth. Set aside to rest for 10 to 15 minutes.
  4. Stir the cheese and eggs into the tapioca mixture until combined; the dough will be thick, like cottage cheese.
  5. Drop the dough in 1/4-cup-sized balls onto an ungreased baking sheet.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until the tops are lightly golden, 15 to 20 minutes.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
