Lasagna


Back to home

Description

A classic Italian comfort food made with layers of lasagna noodles, rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses. Baked until golden and bubbly, it is a hearty dish perfect for family dinners.

Info

  • Total Time: 3 hours and 15 minutes
  • Servings: 12

Ingredients

  • 1 pound sweet Italian sausage
  • ¾ pound lean ground beef
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 (6.5 oz) cans canned tomato sauce
  • 2 (6 oz) cans tomato paste
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 4 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, divided
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons dried basil leaves
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt, divided, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon fennel seeds
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • ¾ pound mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Recipe

  1. Gather all your ingredients.
  2. Cook the sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic in a Dutch oven over medium heat until well browned.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, and water.
    Season with sugar, 2 tablespoons of parsley, basil, 1 teaspoon of salt, Italian seasoning, fennel seeds, and pepper.
    Simmer, covered, for about 1 1/2 hours, stirring occasionally.
  4. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil.
    Cook the lasagna noodles in boiling water for 8 to 10 minutes.
    Drain the noodles and rinse with cold water.
  5. In a bowl, combine the ricotta with the egg, the remaining 2 tablespoons of parsley, and 1/2 teaspoon of salt.
  6. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  7. To assemble, spread 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Arrange 6 noodles lengthwise over the meat sauce, overlapping slightly. Spread with half of the ricotta cheese mixture.
    Top with 1/3 of the mozzarella slices.
    Spoon 1 1/2 cups of meat sauce over the mozzarella and sprinkle with 1/4 cup of Parmesan cheese.
  8. Repeat the layers and top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
    Cover with foil: to prevent sticking, spray the foil with cooking spray or make sure it does not touch the cheese.
  9. Bake in the preheated oven for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 25 minutes.
  10. Let the lasagna rest at room temperature for about 15 minutes before cutting; this helps it set and firm up.
